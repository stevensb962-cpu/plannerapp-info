PlannerApp privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

PlannerApp is a personal, self-hosted application used by its owner. It is not offered as a public service.

Data accessed

With your permission, PlannerApp reads your Google Calendar list and events using the read-only scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly. It uses event times and titles only to schedule tasks around your existing commitments.

Storage

Calendar data and the Google access token are stored only on the private server where PlannerApp runs. They are not uploaded anywhere else.

Sharing

PlannerApp does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising or to train AI models. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke PlannerApp's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or with "Disconnect" in the app's Settings page. Disconnecting deletes the stored token.
